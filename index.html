The Business Case For Venue Gardens

Why Event Gardens Partner With Baraka Supplies

Turn single-use beverages into high-converting marketing tools and premium souvenirs.

1

Exponential Marketing Reach via QR Codes

Every event guest is a potential future client. By putting your venue's custom QR code on the back of each bottle, guests scan to view garden booking packages, Instagram feeds, and virtual tours directly from their phone.

2

Nostalgia & The "Keep-Sake Effect"

Generic beverage bottles are thrown away. Bottles personalized with the couple's portrait, graduate's name, or conference logo become keepsakes that guests carry home and share on social media.

3

Brand Exclusivity (No Third-Party Ads)

Avoid advertising commercial soda or water brands in your high-end venue photos. Custom branded bottles keep all guest photos professional and aligned with your venue's prestige.

4

Versatile Packaging for Any Beverage

Order bulk food-grade PET bottles and package whatever your client's menu requires: fresh juice, iced tea, drinking yoghurt, or pure spring water.
